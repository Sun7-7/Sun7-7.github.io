Research Interests

  • Spiking Learning & Temporal Computing

    Learning and temporal representations in spiking networks, from surrogate gradients to multi-scale encoding and sparse inference.

  • Radar & Intelligent Signal Processing

    Radar sorting, deinterleaving, and recognition in mixed pulse streams, using unsupervised learning, similarity structure, and event-driven representations.

  • Semiconductor Devices & Neuromorphic Hardware

    Power-device design through device physics, TCAD, and data-driven models, alongside interests in neuromorphic device models, neuron arrays, and cross-layer design.
