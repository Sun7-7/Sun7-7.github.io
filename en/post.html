Notes on surrogate-function optimization in spiking neural networks

Background

The spike-generation function is not differentiable, so SNN backpropagation relies on surrogate gradients. The alignment between surrogate shape, width, and the membrane-potential distribution directly influences training stability.

Reading focus

Further notes may develop the motivation, derivations, experimental observations, and reproduction settings of the AAAI 2026 work.

Similarity-guided temporal encoding for radar emitter recognition

Core idea

Radar emitter signals have complex temporal structure. Similarity-guided temporal encoding introduces relationships between samples into spike timing, helping SNNs retain more stable discriminative evidence in noisy conditions.

Reading focus

Further notes may cover preprocessing, encoding modules, loss design, and the experimental settings of the ICASSP 2026 paper.

Spiking self-organizing maps for radar signal deinterleaving

Task perspective

Radar signal deinterleaving seeks to recover the structure of individual emitters from mixed pulse streams. Spiking self-organizing maps offer an unsupervised clustering route compatible with event-driven computation.

Reading focus

Further notes may cover convergence guarantees, competitive learning, and robustness in complex scenarios.

Research directions in ML-assisted semiconductor device modeling

Research direction

Machine learning can build surrogate relationships between device geometry and performance, reducing parameter-sweep and optimization costs. Related work covers LDMOS, U-MOSFET, and SIPOS-modulated drift regions.

Reading focus

Further notes may address data generation, feature design, model selection, and BV/FOM optimization workflows.
