Spiking Learning & Temporal Computing

3 published papers

My work on short-horizon spiking learning addresses temporal representations, training dynamics, and low-latency learning: organizing inputs, adapting surrogate gradients, and transferring knowledge between models with different time horizons.

MTE×STE at ICML combines multi-scale inputs with sparse transform encoding. MPO at AAAI adapts surrogate gradients to membrane-potential distributions. The Neurocomputing study uses mutual distillation between long- and short-step models for low-latency inference. These studies address encoding, gradient optimization, and training mechanisms respectively.

Radar & Intelligent Signal Processing

3 published papers

My radar research studies deinterleaving without labels, encoding and recognition under noise, and cluster decisions across scenarios, using distinct learning mechanisms to recover pulse structure with limited supervision.

Spiking-SOM moves competitive learning and topological organization into the spike domain. SGTE encodes reference similarity as temporal events. The IEEE SPL study combines adaptive clustering with similarity learning for cluster decisions. Related applications include multimodal sensing.

Semiconductor Devices & Neuromorphic Hardware

4 published papers

I investigate power-device design and modeling through the relationships among geometry, electric fields, and model fidelity. A separate interest concerns how device behavior informs neuromorphic models and cross-layer constraints.

Semiconductor Device Design & Modeling

Published studies in APL, IEEE TED, and Micromachines address dielectric trenches, field management, and data-driven models, using the TCAD analyses, surrogate predictions, and device experiments reported in each paper.

Neuromorphic Devices & Cross-layer Design

This separate research interest concerns device models, neuron dynamics, neuron-array modeling, and the relationship between algorithmic and hardware constraints.
