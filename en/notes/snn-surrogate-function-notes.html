Notes on surrogate-function optimization in spiking neural networks

Membrane-potential distributions, surrogate-gradient design, and their role in training stability and gradient propagation.

Background

The spike-generation function is not differentiable, so SNN backpropagation relies on surrogate gradients. The alignment between surrogate shape, width, and the membrane-potential distribution directly influences training stability.

Reading focus

Further notes may develop the motivation, derivations, experimental observations, and reproduction settings of the AAAI 2026 work.
