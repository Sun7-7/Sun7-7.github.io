Spiking self-organizing maps for radar signal deinterleaving

An overview of spiking self-organizing maps for unsupervised radar pulse clustering and deinterleaving.

Task perspective

Radar signal deinterleaving seeks to recover the structure of individual emitters from mixed pulse streams. Spiking self-organizing maps offer an unsupervised clustering route compatible with event-driven computation.

Reading focus

Further notes may cover convergence guarantees, competitive learning, and robustness in complex scenarios.
