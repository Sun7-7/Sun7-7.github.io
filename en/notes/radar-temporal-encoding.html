Similarity-guided temporal encoding for radar emitter recognition

Connections between temporal encoding, similarity constraints, and radar denoising and recognition in SGTE-SNN.

Core idea

Radar emitter signals have complex temporal structure. Similarity-guided temporal encoding introduces relationships between samples into spike timing, helping SNNs retain more stable discriminative evidence in noisy conditions.

Reading focus

Further notes may cover preprocessing, encoding modules, loss design, and the experimental settings of the ICASSP 2026 paper.
