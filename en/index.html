Research Mainlines

Research Position

Neuromorphic computing is my methodological core: understand the mechanism, validate it in real signal applications, and extend the modeling mindset to physically constrained devices.

Line 1

Neuromorphic mechanisms

SNN training stability, surrogate gradients, membrane-potential distributions, temporal coding, and low-step inference.

Line 2

Signal-processing applications

Apply neuromorphic models to continuous 1D signals, radar pulses, remote-sensing images, and multimodal quality sensing under temporal, noise, and edge-inference constraints.

Line 3

Device-aware modeling

Combine TCAD, physical priors, and ML for power-device modeling, with GaN HEMT trench design and electrical measurements for field-management validation.
